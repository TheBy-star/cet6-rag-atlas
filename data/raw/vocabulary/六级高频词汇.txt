## facilitate
【词性】v.
【释义】促进；使便利
【高频搭配】facilitate economic growth / facilitate communication
【例句】The new policy is intended to facilitate economic growth.
【考点】及物动词，后接名词或动名词；不要写成 facilitate to grow。
---
## alleviate
【词性】v.
【释义】减轻，缓解（痛苦、问题）
【高频搭配】alleviate poverty / alleviate pressure / alleviate traffic congestion
【例句】The program aims to alleviate poverty in rural areas.
【考点】比 reduce 更正式，常搭配 poverty、pressure、pain、congestion。
---
## boost
【词性】v. / n.
【释义】促进，推动；增长，提升
【高频搭配】boost economic growth / give a boost to the economy
【例句】These measures will give a strong boost to economic growth.
【考点】“促进经济”优先用 boost economic growth，比 strongly promote 更地道。
---
## curb
【词性】v. / n.
【释义】控制，抑制；路缘
【高频搭配】curb pollution / curb inflation / curb spending
【例句】The government is taking steps to curb air pollution.
【考点】常与 pollution、inflation、crime、spending 搭配，语气比 reduce 更强。
---
## conserve
【词性】v.
【释义】保护，节约（资源、能源）
【高频搭配】conserve energy / conserve water / conserve natural resources
【例句】We should conserve water and energy for future generations.
【考点】conserve 强调“节约使用、保护不被浪费”，不要与 preserve（保存原貌）混淆。
---
## deteriorate
【词性】v.
【释义】恶化，变坏
【高频搭配】deteriorate rapidly / the situation deteriorates
【例句】Air quality may deteriorate rapidly in winter.
【考点】不及物动词；名词 deterioration；写作中可替换 get worse。
---
## diminish
【词性】v.
【释义】减少，削弱
【高频搭配】diminish the impact / diminish in importance
【例句】The policy may diminish the impact of rising costs.
【考点】可及物也可不及物；比 decrease 更偏“逐渐削弱”。
---
## eliminate
【词性】v.
【释义】消除，根除
【高频搭配】eliminate poverty / eliminate discrimination / eliminate the need for
【例句】Education is essential to eliminating poverty.
【考点】语气强，表示“彻底消除”；后接名词或动名词。
---
## enhance
【词性】v.
【释义】提高，增强（质量、能力、体验）
【高频搭配】enhance efficiency / enhance the quality of life / enhance mutual trust
【例句】Digital technology can enhance the efficiency of public services.
【考点】褒义提升，常搭配 quality、efficiency、awareness、trust。
---

---
## hinder

【高频搭配】hinder economic development / hinder progress

---
## implement
【词性】v.
【释义】实施，执行（政策、计划）
【高频搭配】implement a policy / implement reforms / implement measures
【例句】The government will implement a series of reforms next year.

---
## impose

---
## incentive

【高频搭配】provide an incentive for / tax incentives / financial incentives
【例句】Tax incentives encourage companies to invest in green technology.
【考点】可数名词；常用 provide an incentive for sb to do sth。
---
## inevitable

【释义】不可避免的
【高频搭配】an inevitable consequence / it is inevitable that
【例句】Some job losses are an inevitable consequence of automation.
【考点】副词 inevitably；写作常用 It is inevitable that...。
---
## innovate

---
## integrate

---
## mitigate

---
## preserve

【释义】保护，保存（原貌、文化、传统）
【高频搭配】preserve traditional culture / preserve historical sites

---
## promote
【词性】v.
【释义】促进，推动；宣传，晋升

【高频搭配】reinforce the belief / reinforce mutual trust / reinforce stereotypes
【例句】Repeated exposure to false information can reinforce stereotypes.
【考点】常与 belief、trust、stereotype、idea 搭配。
---
## restrain
【词性】v.
【释义】抑制，克制
【高频搭配】restrain spending / restrain emotions / restrain growth
【例句】Higher interest rates may restrain spending.

---
